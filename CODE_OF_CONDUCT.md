# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want F4 Studio to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive open-source project for everyone. Contributors are expected to participate in good faith and to focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
- Explain technical decisions with evidence and patience.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Respect different viewpoints, backgrounds, identities, and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to F4 Studio and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised conduct, doxxing, trolling, spam, malicious code, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports should be made privately through the GitHub security/contact channels listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions when an individual is representing the project.
